DIGITAL ORCHESTRATION
Accelerating the new product introduction process
MenaBev aims to streamline and shorten the end-to-end 
system process for launching new products, thereby 
eliminating manual master data errors and 
accelerating master data creation. The optimized process 
will be more robust and efficient, significantly reducing the 
overall effort required from business users across multiple 
departments in creating and maintaining master data.

=== PAGE 4 ===
INTRODUCTION & REQUIREMENTS
Business Context
MenaBev intends to implement an enterprise-grade AI application to streamline and automate 
end-to-end new product launch activities for business users. The solution will provide a guided 
request initiation process, offering predefined options and data-entry fields for new product 
proposals, thereby ensuring completeness and consistency of submitted information. Upon 
submission, an integrated workflow will automatically route the request to Finance and 
Manufacturing stakeholders, enabling them to review, amend, and enrich department-specific data 
prior to final approval of the product launch.
AI Recommendations
The application will leverage advanced AI capabilities to generate recommendations based on 
curated business datasets and operational data extracted from SAP S/4HANA. These AI-driven 
insights will support more informed decision-making, improve data quality, and accelerate approval 
cycles for new product introductions.
